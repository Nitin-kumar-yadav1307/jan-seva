## 🎬 3-Minute Presentation Flow

### Phase 1: The Problem & The AI Fast Booking (Customer Perspective)
1. **Open the Homepage** ([http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173)).
2. Point out the **Cooperative Trust Bar**: 85% direct worker wages, 10% cooperative welfare/healthcare pool, 100% NSDC certified workers.
3. Click on the **AI Search Bar** or top right **"AI Fast Book"** button.
4. Click the quick prompt: `🚨 Leaking Pipe Emergency` (*"Water pipe burst under kitchen sink, leaking heavily! Need an urgent plumber right now."*).
5. Show the **Supervisor Agent in action**:
   - **Booking Agent** extracts intent (Category: `Plumbing`, Urgency: `HIGH_EMERGENCY`).
   - **Matching Agent** evaluates candidates using the **Deterministic Fairness Engine**.
6. **Highlight the Core Innovation (Explainability)**:
   - Point out that **Ramesh Sharma** is 300m closer, but was de-prioritized because he already logged 48 hours this week.
   - **Suresh Kumar** was selected because he has high NSDC certification, is 1.1 km away, and is well-rested (24 hrs logged), protecting worker welfare and ensuring quality!
7. Click **"Proceed to Book with Suresh Kumar"**.
